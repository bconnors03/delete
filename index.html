BEST FIRST BET

Plant sitters, not banner ads.

Ads pay very little until a lot of people use the app, and they make a calm care routine feel noisy. A sitter marketplace solves a real, occasional problem and can earn a fee when a booking happens.

Recommendation: prove people want the service in one local area before building a full marketplace.

+

Free garden, paid extras

Keep reminders and a small garden free. Test a modest subscription for shared gardens, care history, and trip planning.

↗

Local sitter bookings
